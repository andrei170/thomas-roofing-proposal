One thing to correct

Your margins are not on Companies House, and that's not your fault.

You mentioned they'd be filed there. I checked company 11071653. Every set of accounts since incorporation in 2017 has been filed as total exemption full accounts, which for a company your size means a balance sheet only, with no profit and loss account. Turnover, costs and margin are all legally exempt from public filing.

So the £600k and the margin behind it are numbers only you and your accountant hold. I can't verify them and I'm not going to pretend otherwise. But your accountant can tell you your gross margin per roof in about five minutes, and that one number is what decides how hard we can afford to bid for a customer. It's the most useful thing you could bring to the next conversation.
